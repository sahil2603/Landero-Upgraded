Everything about the code, the leaderboard and claiming rewards.

How do I use code LANDERO111?
Sign up on Stake through the Play Stake button, or enter LANDERO111 as your referral code in your Stake account settings. Every reward on this site needs your account to be under the code.
When does the leaderboard reset?
On the 1st of every month at 00:00 UTC. The countdown on the leaderboard page shows the exact time left.
Why is the leaderboard weighted?
Games with a very low house edge would let people climb by wagering on near-zero-risk bets. Weighting keeps the board fair, so slots count fully and low-edge games count for less.
Can I claim the lossback and the double weekly in the same week?
Yes. Eligible VIP members can claim both. The double weekly is capped at $1,500 and the lossback at $3,000.
When can I claim the weekend specials?
The double weekly opens on Saturday and Sunday. Lossback is requested on Sunday with a screenshot of your weekly result.
Does the rank-up match include recent-play bonuses?
No. Landero matches the rank-up bonus only, as shown in the table on the Rank-Up Match page.
Who can get the 10% deposit bonus?
Players at Silver VIP or higher on Stake. You get 10% of qualifying deposits up to $2,500, which is $250 in bonus funds, followed by a 7-day cooldown.
I'm a highroller. Can the limits go up?
Possibly. DM Landero on Discord and he can work something out on the limits.
How long do claims take?
Claims are reviewed by Landero and the mods. Open a ticket in the Discord with your claim and a screenshot, and you will be updated there.

Still stuck?

Open a ticket in the Discord and the mods will help you out.
